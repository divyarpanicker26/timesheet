Special Bi-Weekly Alternate Time SheetNYCHA 015.210A
Who uses it

Three sign-ins, three jobs

Employee

Fills the bi-weekly form, draws a signature, and submits. Sees only their own sheets.

Supervisor

Reviews every sheet, checks the weekday pattern, and approves or sends it back.

Admin

Creates user ids and chooses who can submit, approve, or manage users.

The form

The weekday pattern

  • Weekdays through the form date are 9.00AM–5.00PM.
  • Saturday, Sunday, and future days stay blank.
  • The reason is Approved remote work.
